Recording tips

Read one passage in your natural accent and the style you want for narration. Use a quiet room, a steady microphone distance, and no music or other speakers. Stop when you finish; there is no need to fill the time.

Browsing passages keeps your transcript. The button replaces it. If you stumble, record again or edit the transcript to match exactly. For an uploaded recording, use its actual words.

Saving a sample does not approve its generated voice. Listen to an audition, then accept it before producing.
