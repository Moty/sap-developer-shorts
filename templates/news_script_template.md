# [NEWS-NN] [One clear angle]

Format: news. Audience: [who needs this]. Event or coverage: [name]. Sources checked: [actual date]. Editorial selection: [why these stories matter to that audience]. Target: 45–60 seconds. Review window: at most seven days. Status: editorial draft.

## Source review

For every material announcement, record:

| Field | Required content |
| --- | --- |
| Claim | A concise paraphrase, with vendor attribution where needed |
| Official source | Exact HTTPS SAP URL and title |
| Dates | Published date if supplied; actual source-read and claim-check date |
| Support | Section heading and a short paraphrase of the supporting detail |
| Availability | Generally available, rollout, early access, planned, or announced only |
| Timing and scope | Release window, geography, eligibility, dependencies, or unconfirmed details |

## Seven scenes

| Scene | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Hook | One reason to care, at most seven words in three seconds |
| Context | Name the event, time frame, and editorial angle |
| Announcement 1 | What changed; reference one reviewed claim |
| Announcement 2 | What changed; reference one reviewed claim |
| Announcement 3 | What changed; reference one reviewed claim |
| Takeaway | Label your interpretation and give a practical next step |
| CTA | Point to the official sources or ask which topic to explain next |

Keep planned dates conditional in speech, cards, and companion copy. Do not imply a product was tested. Summarize in original language, keep quotations short, and do not copy an article or its images into the video. Use `content/episodes/NEWS-01.json` as the machine-readable example.

## Before production

Re-read expired sources, review whether the text supports each claim, confirm availability wording, measure the narration, inspect captions and card readability, and review the exact companion post. A local narrated storyboard remains a preview until final avatar media, video export, and editorial approval are complete.
