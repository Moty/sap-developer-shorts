From idea to published: making SAP Shorts feel like one product

I walked every surface of the current studio at 1440px and 390px wide, using the real local library. This page covers what gets in the way, the information architecture I'd move to, and the specific changes for each screen. Each change links to a clickable mockup with desktop and phone layouts.

Summary

What already works

  • A clear lifecycle: Ideas → Editing → Producing → To review → To publish → Published.
  • The facelifted editor: scene outline, one focused scene and a live phone cue.
  • Strong trust and safety: dated source windows, an exact-MP4 approval, local-only data and explicit cost consent.
  • A calm, consistent green palette in the workspace, plus usable phone layouts.

The five moves I'd prioritise

  1. Group versions under stories. The home list shows stories, with versions in a drawer. Name versions v1…vN with an optional note.
  2. Give each episode one workspace. Script → Voice & presenter → Produce → Review & publish share one header and stepper.
  3. Make creation a first-class action. A global “New short” opens a single sheet: source, format, tone, length and narrator.
  4. Lead with what needs you. The home screen opens with review, retry and expiring-source cards, plus a stage pipeline.
  5. Use one design system on every surface. The desktop launcher, private workspace and deck use the same tokens and section names. Settings and disclaimers live in one place each.

Cross-cutting findings

High

Versions are shown as separate shorts

The library lists 62 rows for 9 stories. The same title appears several times, for example “SAP finance: follow the money” twice on page 1. Slug names grow without limit: NEWS-01@my-edit-edit-edit-edit-new1-edit. With 8 rows per page, a creator pages through duplicates to find work.

Evidence: library manifest (CAP-01 has 23 versions, NEWS-01 has 12). My shorts page 1 of 8.
High

Each episode spreads across five page types with different chrome

These are edit.html (top bar and dark rail), publish.html (only “← Workspace”), watch.html (“← All episodes”, its own header, banner shown twice), video/review.html (“← All episodes and styles”), and presentation.html (a dark theme). Back links, titles and layout change on every hop, and the same generation-time block appears on three pages.

Evidence: screenshots 07, 10, 11 and 09. The freshness banner is rendered by both the wrapper and the embedded review (src/news/freshness.ts).
High

There is no primary way to create a short

Each page header offers “Explore ideas ↗”, even on the Ideas page. The ↗ suggests an external link, but it goes to an in-page anchor. Creation exists only as six identical dark buttons on news cards.

Medium

Four visual languages, and a fifth set of section names

The workspace is light green, the editor rail is dark green, the deck is near-black and teal, and the desktop launcher is navy and lime. The launcher is the very first touchpoint, so the brand changes before you reach the studio. The private studio matches the palette but uses its own header, components and section names.

Medium

Settings and warnings are duplicated

Provider, model, budgets and the API key appear in Writing engine and in the editor’s Writing & visuals pane. The Hyperspace cost warning appears in three places, each with its own consent checkbox that disables the buttons beside it. Produce repeats “target 45–60s, 15–90s accepted” twice in consecutive sentences.

Medium

Unclear controls and status

Several controls are disabled with no reason given: Voice library actions before a voice is selected, a primary-styled “Use automatic presentation design”, “Upload Photo”, and “Approve”. The status reads as two stacked fragments, “Unsaved changes / Draft checked”. The main button label changes in every pane: “Continue to voice”, “Review & produce”, “Ready to produce”, “Back to script”.

Medium

Long jobs give no feedback

A short takes about 10 minutes to render (audio 1m 25s, portrait 8m 38s), but no progress screen, ETA or completion notice exists. Status shows only as a “Producing” count on the home list. The 15–90s timing change also left older versions “Ready to retry” without a clear path.

Medium

Phones bury the content

The section tabs overflow (“Writi…”). The stage tiles, type filter, attention checkbox and sort/per-page/page selects push the first short to about 1,000px down. Native selects are used for pagination.

Low

Technical details reach the creator

Examples include portrait names such as d29f457b-4dcd-…png, “Score: 113” in monospace, selectors with “@”, titles cut mid-phrase (“…Agents to Advance”), and terminal alternatives next to buttons.

Low

Accessibility and polish

Issues include emoji icons (“🔊 Audition Line”), an unstyled range input, 11px faint text in the dark rail, and a checkbox plus a disabled button for one approval. The redesign keeps visible labels, at least 32px targets, focus rings, reduced-motion support and dark mode.

Each card shows the current screen, the main issues and what changes. “Open mockup” goes to the matching screen in the prototype. Switch between Desktop and Phone in its top bar.

Desktop app launcher

Set up this Mac

Issues
  • A separate navy/lime brand.
  • Four “Checking” chips, and no overall progress or required/optional split.
  • The main button looks disabled while tools are checked.
  • The activity log sits beside the main actions.
Change
  • A progress ring (3/4) and a list of tools marked required or optional, with download sizes.
  • Installs run inline with an ETA.
  • “Start your first short” works immediately, because writing doesn’t need the render models.
  • The log moves into a disclosure.

Private workspace

Sign in

Issues
  • A single centred card with its own header and components, rather than the studio’s.
  • When configuration is missing, it shows the raw text “Client error: Script error.”
  • Its sections use different names (“Scripts & videos”, “SAP News Discovery”, “My photos”).
Change
  • A split brand panel and a form, built from the same tokens.
  • The same section names as the local studio, with photos under Voices & presenters.
  • Errors say what is wrong and what to do next.

review.html · My shorts

Shorts (home)

Issues
  • 62 version rows, with duplicate titles and slug names.
  • A 240px hero that repeats on every section.
  • Large stage tiles, and pagination in a heavy grey panel.
  • “More” opens a disclaimer essay.
  • Unrendered drafts show a “NEWS” placeholder.
Change
  • Needs you cards for review, retry and new stories.
  • A compact pipeline filter with counts.
  • One row per story, with a versions drawer (v8 … v4, note, stage, length, voice, Watch, Compare).
  • A list or board (kanban) view, and an active job shown in the sidebar.

Ideas & SAP News Hub

Ideas → New short sheet

Issues
  • Three stacked headings say the same thing.
  • A raw score in monospace.
  • The same suggested angle on every card.
  • Six equally heavy primary buttons.
  • No signal that a story already has a short (NEWS-06 exists).
Change
  • A URL bar as the quickest way in.
  • A five-step fit meter, a distinct angle per story, and an “Already a short · NEWS-06 · 8 versions” badge.
  • “Start short” opens the New short sheet, where you pick source, format, tone, length and narrator, then Create draft.

Delivery styles

Formats

Issues
  • Mixes a format catalogue with a CAP pilot demo.
  • Status lines read like build output (“Script prepared · preview pending”).
  • Tone tabs wrap onto a second row.
Change
  • A gallery with a 9:16 example, “best for”, scene rhythm bars and “Use format”.
  • A “Same story, four tones” comparison.
  • Formats also appear as the picker in the New short sheet.

Voice library

Voices & presenters

Issues
  • Personal voices sit behind a select box, with three disabled actions until one is chosen.
  • Only 2 stock voices are visible, and 28 are hidden.
  • The default narrator shows as a disabled button.
  • Presenters exist only inside each episode.
Change
  • Playable cards with waveforms, a default star and inline Approve or Install.
  • A clear “needs your listen” callout.
  • A Presenters gallery with friendly names and alignment status.
  • A two-step Add a voice style sheet: record, then listen and approve.

Writing engine

Settings

Issues
  • A main-navigation slot for a setting you configure once.
  • The same panel is repeated in the editor.
  • Rendering tools live only in the desktop launcher.
  • No view of spend.
Change
  • Settings has four sections: Writing engine, This Mac, Storage & data, Publishing.
  • Providers are radio cards marked “Free” or “May incur cost”.
  • A single consent policy (once per session, every request, or never).
  • Recent requests with cost.

edit.html · Script + Episode details + Writing & visuals

Episode · 1 Script

Issues
  • Episode details is its own pane with two fields and a disabled primary-styled button.
  • The writing assistant repeats engine settings and needs a cost checkbox per request.
  • The timing line is plain text (“5 spoken words · 3s planned”).
  • Emoji buttons.
Change
  • A scene rail with timing bars, flagged in amber when over.
  • Inline assistant chips (punchier, shorten, simplify, fact-check).
  • A word and time meter, plus a Checks panel with “Suggest trim” and “Go to scene”.

edit.html · Voice & portrait

Episode · 2 Voice & presenter

Issues
  • Every choice is a select box.
  • The portrait is named by UUID filename.
  • A large primary button for the optional “Detect face”.
  • A native blue slider.
  • Four nested disclosures, and pronunciation hidden at the bottom.
Change
  • Two cards side by side.
  • Voice: playable narrator cards that “hear scene 1”, segmented delivery and pronunciation chips.
  • Presenter: a large portrait with the mouth outline and thumbnails to switch.
  • Render modes show their time cost (~9 min, ~1 min or slides only), with a styled movement slider.

edit.html · Review & produce (post · AI review · produce)

Episode · 3 Produce

Issues
  • Produce is the last of three tabs.
  • Five paragraphs of overlapping rules.
  • AI review sits in its own tab, with a consent checkbox and three buttons.
  • No ETA, and nothing to watch during the ~10-minute render.
Change
  • A pre-flight checklist (Script, Voice, Presenter, Sources, Companion post), each with Edit.
  • AI review as an optional row (“Run with Gemma · free”).
  • An ETA from history and “Save & produce v8”.
  • Then staged progress you can leave, with a notification when done.
Open mockups: Produce · progress view via the sidebar job card or “Save & produce”
publish.html + watch.html + video/review.html

Episode · 4 Review & publish

Issues
  • Three pages show the same video.
  • Download & post looks active before approval.
  • Approval asks for one generic checkbox.
  • The companion post is a small textarea, and Copy is a text link.
  • Generation time is an indented list.
Change
  • One page: a player with chapter markers, plus Transcript, Sources and Details tabs (with a timing bar).
  • Approval uses four concrete checks (speech, lip-sync, captions, facts).
  • Steps 2–3 stay locked until approval.
  • A LinkedIn-style preview with a character count and Copy, then save the live link.

presentation.html

Deck preview

Issues
  • A separate dark theme and its own back link.
  • Reached only from a “Presentation ↗” link on each row.
  • A large empty area in the phone frame.
Change

  • Only the stage is dark, so you keep context and the version switcher.
